# Recipe sources and licenses

The 500 recipes in `data/meat-recipes.mjs` are selected from the Wikibooks Cookbook snapshot collected on 2024-07-31 by Gosse Minnema. Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/gossminn/wikibooks-cookbook . Snapshot file: https://huggingface.co/datasets/gossminn/wikibooks-cookbook/resolve/main/recipes_parsed.mini.json .

Recipe text is by Wikibooks contributors and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. The complete license is bundled in `LICENSE-RECIPES-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`; see https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ and https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Wikibooks:Copyrights . The same CC BY-SA 4.0 license applies to this normalized recipe-text collection. This does not change licenses for independently authored app code or separately credited photos.

Changes: selected 500 distinct sources with ingredient lists and substantial methods; removed navigation/introductory notes; normalized ingredient names for pantry matching; retained source ingredient measurements and methods verbatim. Structured quantities are null for imported lines; the app displays the exact source text rather than guessing numbers. Original time and servings are retained; absent values are shown as unspecified. A country is assigned only when the source title names it; otherwise cuisine is International. The recipes are community-authored source material, not a claim of professional kitchen validation.

Selection excludes synthetic records, obvious raw-meat dishes, fermentation condiments and cured-meat methods, stock/sauce-only meat matches, ambiguous alternative main meats, and exact duplicate methods. The source URL, contributor-history link, license and original ingredient lines are bundled per recipe and displayed in the detail view. Admin edits retain attribution and mark the recipe as edited.

Imported dishes without a verified photo use a labeled placeholder. They do not borrow a photograph of another dish. Existing 40 Indian home recipes and their credited photos remain available.
